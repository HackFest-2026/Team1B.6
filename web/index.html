From satellite to debris
1 · CommissioningFirst months. Full propellant, can maneuver.
2 · OperationalStation-keeping. Collision-avoidance burns come out of the propellant budget.
3 · Late lifeOver 60 % of design life used. Every burn is weighed against the disposal reserve.
4 · End of lifeDesign life over or only the disposal reserve is left. It can no longer dodge: it is debris in all but name.
5 · Disposal or derelictA good end: de-orbit burn, or a graveyard orbit. A failed one stays in orbit as debris.
